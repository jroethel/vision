## Additional Substring Messages

Many additional messages that support substring operations are defined for the class *String*:

The following messages return a **substring** of the recipient:

**Message**

**Definition**

**Sample**

[*from:to:*](../../messages/mString/from-to.md)

Extracts substring from position *parameter1* to position *parameter2*

"abcdef" from: 2 to: 4 .

[*from:for:*](../../messages/mString/from-for.md)

Extracts substring from position *parameter1* for number of characters indicated by *parameter2*

"abcdef" from: 2 for: 3 .

[*from:*](../../messages/mString/from.md)

If *parameter* is a number, extracts substring from this position to end; if *parameter* is a string, extracts from first occurrence to end

"abcdef" from: "b" .

[*to:*](../../messages/mString/to.md)

If *parameter* is a number, extracts substring from beginning to this position; if *parameter* is a string, extracts from beginning to its first occurrence

"abcdef" to: "b" .

The following messages are **inquiries** about the recipient and return a *Boolean* value:

**Message**

**Definition**

**Sample**

[*isBlank*](../../messages/mString/isBlank.md)

Is *recipient* blank?

" abc" isBlank

[*isBlankFor:*](../../messages/mString/isBlankFor.md)

Are the first *parameter* characters of *recipient* blank?

" abc" isBlankFor: 2 .

[*contains:*](../../messages/mString/contains.md)

Does *recipient* contain *parameter* as a substring? (wildcards are recognized)

"whatAmI" contains: "^what" .

[*containsSubstring:*](../../messages/mString/containsSubstring.md)

Does *recipient* contain *parameter* as a substring? (wildcards viewed as literals)

"whatAmI" containsSubstring: "^what"

The following messages return integer values representing a **position** or a **length**:

**Message**

**Definition**

**Sample**

[*findPatternExtent:*](../../messages/mString/findPatternExtent.md)

Length of *parameter* if present in *recipient*, interpretting wildcards

"abcdef" findPatternExtent: "\[a-c\]\[a-c\]" .

[*findSubstringExtent:*](../../messages/mString/findSubstringExtent.md)

Length of *parameter* if present in *recipient*, viewing wildcards as literals

"abcdef" findSubstringExtent: "\[a-c\]\[a-c\]" .

[*findPatternOrigin:*](../../messages/mString/findPatternOrigin.md)

Zero-based position of first occurrence of *parameter* in *recipient*, interpretting wildcards

"ab^c" findPatternOrigin: "^c" .

[*findSubstringOrigin:*](../../messages/mString/findSubstringOrigin.md)

Zero-based position of first occurrence of *parameter* in *recipient*, viewing wildcards as literals

"ab^c" findSubstringOrigin: "^c" .

[*findPatternSuffix:*](../../messages/mString/findPatternSuffix.md)

Zero-based position of first character after first occurrence of *parameter* in *recipient*, interpretting wildcards

"ab^cd" findPatternSuffix: "^c" .

[*findSubstringSuffix:*](../../messages/mString/findSubstringSuffix.md)

Zero-based position of first character after first occurrence of *parameter* in *recipient*, viewing wildcards as literals

"ab^cd" findSubstringSuffix: "^c" .

[*startingPositionOf:*](../../messages/mString/startingPositionOf.md)

Position of first occurrence of *parameter* in *recipient*

"abc" startingPositionOf: "bc" .

[*startingPositionsOf:*](../../messages/mString/startingPositionsOf.md)

List of position numbers at which *parameter* starts in *recipient*

"abcdbcebc" startingPositionsOf: "bc" .

[*prefixNotSpannedBy:*](../../messages/mString/prefixNotSpannedBy.md)

Number of characters at start of *recipient*that do not match any character in *parameter*

"abc" prefixNotSpannedBy: "cdefg" .

[*prefixSpannedBy:*](../../messages/mString/prefixSpannedBy.md)

Number of characters at start of *recipient* that match any character in *parameter*

"abc" prefixSpannedBy: "aeiou" .
