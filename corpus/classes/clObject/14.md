<span id="classAccess"></span>

The following messages are used to determine the recipient's [**class inheritance**](../../general/Classes/8.md):

**Message**

**Definition**

[*showInheritance*](../../messages/mObject/showInheritance.md)

Displays inheritance hierarchy of *recipient*

[*inheritsFrom:*](../../messages/mObject/inheritsFrom_.md)

Is *recipient* a subclass of *parameter*?

[*isSuperClassOf:*](../../messages/mObject/isSuperClassOf_.md)

Is *recipient* a superclass of *parameter*?
