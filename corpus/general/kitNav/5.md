### Full Class Hierarchy Report

The **Full Class Hierarchy Report** is one of the report views that allows you to navigate to an initial class. This report shows all the available classes in hierarchical form starting with *Object* as the top of the hierarchy. Classes at the same level in the hierarchy are sorted alphabetically. You can can click on a class to display its summary report (i.e., the [*Class Summary*](#ClassSummaryFor) report).
