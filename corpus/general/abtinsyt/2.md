## Who We Are

Insyte, founded in 1981, designs and installs database management and decision support systems using Vision<sup>TM</sup>, our object-oriented, analytical database management technology. As both a technology developer and a systems implementer, we uniquely offer clients access to our Vision technology and the direct involvent of our highly capable staff. We have installed mission critical, production systems for clients in such industries as investment management, international trade, health care and media research.
