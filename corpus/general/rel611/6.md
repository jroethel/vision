- s tokenizeOnPattern: "[Tt]he". do: ["<" print; print; ">" printNL];

had been written as:

- s tokenizeOnPattern: "[Tt].*e".do: ["<" print; print; ">" printNL];

the resulting output would have been:

- <>
      <The quick brown fox jumped over the>
      < lazy dog.>

instead of:

- <The>
      < quick brown fox jumped over >
      <the>
      < lazy dog.>
