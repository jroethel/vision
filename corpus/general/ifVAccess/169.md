#### Description:

> A read-only property whose value is the kind of date list held in this extract workspace.  Three kinds of date list are possible:
>
> 1.  a date list built one entity at a time using a sequence of calls to the *[AddDate](#method%20ExtractWS::AddDate)* method,
> 2.  a date list specified as a comma separated list of entity names (*[DateListString](#property%20ExtractWS::DateListString)*),
> 3.  a date list dynamically derived using a Vision expression (*[DateListExpression](#property%20ExtractWS::DateListExpression)*).
