#### Description:

> A property and associated, alternative update method that specified the Vision expression used to generate a dynamic list of entities in entity by time or time queries.  The expression will be evaluated by the entity identified by the *[Entity](#property%20ExtractWS::Entity)* and *[EntityType](#property%20ExtractWS::EntityType)* properties of this workspace.
