#### Description:

> A method that returns the elements of a *DatumArray* as an array of doubles.  Elements not representable as doubles are returned as the *[DoubleNaN](#property%20FormatSettings::DoubleNaN)* value supplied by this *DatumArray*'s *[FormatSettings](#property%20DatumArray::FormatSettings)* object.
