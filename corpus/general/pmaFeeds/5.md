## Starter Feeds

To successfully use the supplied [*Headstart Applications*](../pmaApps.md), your initial feeds should include security, portfolio, and company masters, holdings data, and supplemental data such as industry and sector masters. The recommended starter feeds are described in the document, [*Portfolio Management Application Startup*](../pmaInstall/5.md). These feeds provide enough information to use most of the starter portfolio reports without modification.
