### Considerations: Industry Scheme

**Question:**

- Do you want to be able to see your data grouped into industries and sectors?

**Answer:** *Yes*

- You should consider adding industry and sector information to your database. This requires that you:
  - supply the [**SectorMaster**](../../feeds/pma-SectorMaster.md) and [**IndustryMaster**](../../feeds/pma-IndustryMaster.md) feeds with the fields *entityId* and *name*.
  - supply the [**IndustryToSector**](../../feeds/pma-IndustryToSector.md) feed to map industries into sectors.
  - supply the [**CompanyToIndustry**](../../feeds/pma-CompanyToIndustry.md) feed to map companies into industries.
