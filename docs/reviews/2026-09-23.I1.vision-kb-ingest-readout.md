3. G3: the corpus is text-only, and just 3 image references survive.
4. G4: ingest selects only `*.htm` files.
5. G5: the shredder splits at every heading, which orphans headings like `## Available Fields:` from the tables below them.
6. G6: 149 links point at 14 pages absent from the snapshot, and 79 point at anchors absent from their own source page.
